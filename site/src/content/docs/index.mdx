A hands-on C#/.NET guide for **Node.js + TypeScript** developers. It covers the **key differences** between the two stacks — the other ~70% you already know. Every topic has **concepts** (theory with TypeScript comparisons) and **hands-on exercises** (build, break, and fix real code). Topics 5–10 build one **Payment API** step by step.
